The stations: how you get in

Every stop is a real screen in the test version. Nothing here asks for your papers, your face or your money.

  1. 1 Create a key

    Gloki has no passwords. One tap makes your key and signs you in.

    Right now the key works like a password: whoever types it in is you. We say so on the screen where you make it.

  2. 2 Save your key

    It is the only way back into your account. Gloki cannot reset it, so you copy it or download it before you go on.

  3. 3 Say what you are

    A person, an organisation, or someone who will host a node. An organisation is declared, not checked, in this test.

  4. 4 Four people vouch for you

    People who know you confirm you are a real person. That, and nothing you pay or upload, is what makes your voice count. You ask someone you know; a video call and a daily session are there for the rest.

  5. 5 Verified

    Verified everywhere on Gloki, and able to vouch for the next person. Four approvals is the whole of it.

  6. 6 The forum

    One conversation shared by everyone in the test. Post, reply, like. Your name and your country's flag, not a code.

The Global Democracy Initiative

Assume global responsibility

The Global Democracy Initiative began in 2021, when seven organisations working for global democracy agreed on a plan. People anywhere would name the problems no single country can fix, propose solutions, debate them and vote: one person, one vote. The results would be public, so that the people in power can be held to them.

A plan like that needs one thing first: knowing that each vote belongs to one real person, without asking any government for papers. In 2023 the Initiative's identity working group wrote that up as Gloki, a global voter identity.

Gloki is that plan, being built. The test version starts with the simplest part: people who know you vouch for you.

  • Global democracy. Citizens can take part directly in global decisions.
  • Global citizenship. Our common humanity binds us together, as one diverse human species.
  • Open source innovation. We build new ways for people to cooperate, decide and act on global issues, and we share them with the world.

Where it started: the 2021 concept and the 2023 Gloki white paper (PDF). The test version has moved on from some of the paper's ideas, and the status board above is the current word.

The Global Democracy Initiative is a project of the Young World Federalists, a nonprofit registered in the United States that hosts it legally and financially.
