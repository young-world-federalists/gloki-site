## Part C: Transnational / large-scale e-democracy landscape

Identity / one-person-one-vote (1p1v) mechanism is in **bold** for each project.

**C1. Decidim** (https://decidim.org): VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/decidim/decidim/develop/README.adoc).
- **What it is:** "Decidim is a participatory democracy framework, written in Ruby on Rails, originally developed for the Barcelona City government online and offline participation website."
- **Identity: pluggable, set by each operator.** Verbatim: "This tries to solve the problem of how to verify that the user is who they say they are and that they have the right to participate in this city or organization." Also: "You can easily add any authentication provider to Decidim that is provided by OmniAuth."
- **Hosting:** open-source (AGPL), self-hosted by each city or organisation. Active.

**C2. Consul Democracy** (https://consuldemocracy.org): VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/consuldemocracy/consuldemocracy/master/README.md, plus https://raw.githubusercontent.com/consuldemocracy/docs/master/en/features/census_configuration.md).
- **What it is:** "Citizen Participation and Open Government Application"; "originally developed for the Madrid City government eParticipation website".
- **Identity: an email account, plus checks against the municipal census (resident register) before voting.** Verbatim: "for some actions like voting, you will need a verified user". Also: "Currently the application was designed to send only the **document number** and **document type**." The census doc concerns "the connection with the Town Hall Census".
- **Hosting:** open-source, self-hosted per municipality. Active.
- **Caveat:** the docs repo is now read-only and has moved to docs.consuldemocracy.org.

**C3. Polis** (https://pol.is): description VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/compdemocracy/polis/edge/README.md); identity UNVERIFIED.
- **What it is:** "Polis is an AI powered sentiment gathering platform. More organic than surveys and less effort than focus groups."
- **Hosting:** "the main deployment of Polis is at https://pol.is, and is free to use for nonprofits and government"; also self-hostable.
- **Identity: open/anonymous voting by default; optional social login or an external ID ("XID").** The README says only "Polis uses JWT keys for participant authentication." The identity guide (https://compdemocracy.org/Identity) was not fetched; WebSearch summary: participants are "allowed to vote without logging in". **No 1p1v guarantee found.**

**C4. Vocdoni** (https://vocdoni.io): VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vocdoni/vocdoni-node/main/README.md and https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vocdoni/vocdoni-sdk/main/README.md).
- **What it is:** "Vocdoni is the first universally verifiable, censorship-resistant, anonymous, and self-sovereign governance protocol." Also: "We are engineering building blocks for a permissionless, private and censorship resistant democracy."
- **Identity: an organiser-defined census (electoral roll) of cryptographic addresses; anonymity is a per-election option.** Verbatim from the SDK: "A process will require a census of people who will vote it." Code comments: `// accepts any ethereum-alike addresses` and `anonymous: false, // if the election is anonymous`.
- **Hosting:** its own blockchain ("Vocdoni chain"), plus a SaaS app at vocdoni.app. Active.

**C5. Loomio** (https://www.loomio.com): description VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/loomio/loomio/master/README.md).
- **What it is:** "Loomio is open-source software for making decisions together."
- **Hosting:** "Start a free trial… Alternatively Set up your own Loomio server" (SaaS or self-hosted).
- **Identity: group membership managed by group admins (email accounts).** Not stated in the README, so this point is UNVERIFIED. Aimed at groups and organisations, not mass democracy.

**C6. Talk to the City (AI Objectives Institute)**: VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AIObjectives/tttc-light-js/main/README.md).
- **What it is:** "Talk to the City (T3C) is an open-source, LLM-enabled SaaS tool for improving collective deliberation and decision-making by analyzing detailed, qualitative data."
- **Identity: none.** It analyses responses collected elsewhere and is not a voting system.
- **Hosting:** SaaS (talktothe.city) on Firebase/GCS. Active. The older repo says it "will soon be made read-only and archived."

**C7. Global Citizens' Assembly** (https://globalassemblies.org; note the "assemblies" spelling, not globalassembly.org): UNVERIFIED (site not fetched).
- **Identity: sortition** (selection by lottery). WebSearch summary of secondary sources: a population-weighted location lottery (Sept 2025), then a demographic lottery. 105 members were chosen in Jan 2026; 14 online sessions ran 17 Jan to 14 Mar 2026 on food and climate.
- **Hosting:** an NGO-run assembly, not an app.

**C8. Democracy Earth** (https://democracy.earth): VERIFIED; **appears inactive** (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DemocracyEarth/sovereign/master/README.md).
- **What it is:** "Governance for Digital Autonomous Organizations." "Built for Ethereum using Web3." "Currently version 0.9."
- **Identity: an Ethereum wallet / token holdings** (MolochDAO contracts; ERC20 and ERC721 tokens). **No 1p1v.**
- **Hosting:** Ethereum plus IPFS. The latest screenshot is dated 2020, and the dapp is still at version 0.9.

### Identity layers often used with these tools

**C9. World ID (Worldcoin / Tools for Humanity)**: VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/worldcoin/world-id-protocol/main/README.md).
- **What it is:** "World ID is a protocol built to enable anonymous proof of human (PoH) at global scale and to complement existing identity systems." Also: "World ID allows individuals to prove things about themselves — like they are a real and unique human, not a bot — without revealing any personal information."
- **Identity: biometric (iris scan by "Orb" device).** This is not stated in the README; it is from general knowledge and UNVERIFIED here.
- **Hosting:** company-run, plus blockchain smart contracts.

**C10. Human Passport (formerly Gitcoin Passport)**: VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/passportxyz/passport/main/README.md).
- **What it is:** "Passport is an identity verification application."
- **Identity: stacked verifiable credentials ("stamps").** Verbatim: "We have written software enabling people to grow personal collections of verifiable credentials about themselves…" Also: "we are actively exploring its benefits for personhood proofs".

**C11. BrightID**: VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BrightID/BrightID/master/README.md).
- **What it is:** "BrightID allows you to prove to applications that you're only using one account."
- **Identity: social-graph vouching.** Verbatim: "No personal data or biometrics are used for verification." Also: "Verified by a trusted group of close personal contacts plus a decentralized graph database."
- **Hosting:** volunteer-run nodes. "The network consists of volunteers running nodes".

**C12. Proof of Humanity (Kleros)**: VERIFIED only that the app exists (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Proof-Of-Humanity/proof-of-humanity-web/master/README.md: "This is the Proof of Humanity web app you will find at https://app.proofofhumanity.id.").
- **Identity: video, a deposit and vouching, with disputes settled by Kleros.** Not in the README, so UNVERIFIED.

**C13. Reticulum** (https://reticulum.network): VERIFIED (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/markqvist/Reticulum/master/README.md, the author's own repo, described as "a public mirror").
- **What it is:** "Reticulum is the cryptography-based networking stack for building local and wide-area networks with readily available hardware."
- **Not a democracy app.** Identity is self-generated key pairs: "Initiator anonymity, communicate without revealing your identity".

**Not checked (no primary source reachable):** Make.org, Democracy Next, Stanford Deliberative Democracy Lab / Meta Community Forums, LiquidFeedback, vTaiwan/Join, Remesh, Frankly, Agora.

### Factual summary of identity / 1p1v approaches found
- **Government or census verification:** Consul (municipal census, document number). Decidim (pluggable authorizations, often census or eID).
- **Organiser-defined voter roll of keys, with optional anonymity:** Vocdoni (census of addresses on its own chain).
- **Open or weak login, no 1p1v guarantee:** Polis (anonymous voting, optional social login/XID). Talk to the City (no identity; analysis only).
- **Group membership managed by admins:** Loomio.
- **Token or wallet weighting (not 1p1v):** Democracy Earth (Ethereum, appears inactive).
- **Proof of personhood:** biometric (World ID); social-graph vouching (BrightID); credential stacking (Human Passport); vouching plus dispute (Proof of Humanity).
- **Sortition:** Global Citizens' Assembly.
