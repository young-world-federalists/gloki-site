# Persona review of the GDI home-page copy drafts (26 Oct 2026)

Shared brief for every reviewer. Your own prompt gives you your persona.

## What you are reviewing

Three competing drafts of the home-page copy for globaldemocracyinitiative.org. The founder will
merge them into one page, so your review decides what survives.

- `docs/copy/draft-a-broken-promise.md` (angle: the 1990s promise the internet broke)
- `docs/copy/draft-b-one-person-one-vote.md` (angle: the missing piece for one person, one vote)
- `docs/copy/draft-c-keep-the-promise.md` (angle: technology broke this, technology has to fix it)

All paths are relative to `/home/user/gloki-site`. The locked positioning is
`docs/positioning-gdi.md`. Read it **after** your first pass, so your first reaction is a real
reader's. Bracketed `[PLACEHOLDER]` text stands for a quote, number or link still to be verified;
judge whether the sentence works once it is filled, and don't penalise the bracket itself. Only
read the page copy (section 2 of each draft). Ignore each draft's notes and claims-check table:
readers won't see them.

## How to review

1. **First 60 seconds, per draft.** Read only the hero and the first section, as your persona would
   on a phone, arriving from a link someone sent you. Write in the persona's voice, 2–3 sentences:
   what you think this is, whether you keep reading, and which door you would pick (or none).
2. **Full read, per draft.** Read the whole page copy. Note where you get lost, bored, suspicious or
   convinced. Quote the exact sentence each time.
3. **Rank** A, B and C for you, with one line of why.
4. **Findings.** Your top issues across all drafts, each with:
   - severity: **blocker** (would make you leave or distrust the project), **major** (weakens the
     page for you), **minor**;
   - the exact quoted text and which draft it comes from;
   - what would fix it, in one line. Suggest wording only if it is short.
5. **Keep list.** The 3–5 sentences or sections, from any draft, that must survive the merge.
6. **Would you act?** Yes or no, which door, and the one thing that would change your answer.

## Rules

- Stay in persona for steps 1, 2 and 6. Steps 3–5 can be in plain reviewer voice.
- Don't rewrite the page. Findings, not a new draft.
- Don't invent facts about the project. If you want to know something the copy doesn't say, write
  it as a question the page should answer.
- Write your review to `docs/reviews/2026-10-26-copy-personas/<your-file>.md` (name given in your
  prompt). Keep it under about 900 words. Touch no other file and don't commit.
- Return a summary of at most 8 lines: your ranking, your one blocker (or "none"), and whether
  you would act.
