## Locked
- Publisher: Global Democracy Initiative (GDI), a project of Young World Federalists, Inc.
- **Gloki = the foundation**: one identity per person (people who know you vouch for you), your own
  agent that keeps your records, a network its members carry. The democracy app comes later, on
  top; don't name it.
- Promise (use verbatim, may be split across h1 + subline): "There will be no global democracy
  without technology. We are building the part every democracy app is missing: one real person,
  one vote, on a network its members carry."
- Door order: (1) Solve global issues — world federalists, academics, future members; (2) Build
  digital democracy — people building democracy apps, civic tech, researchers; (3) Carry the
  network — people with a server (door only records interest; no node software yet, say so).
- Waitlist: separate GDI list. Sign-ups never get the YWF newsletter; say that next to the form.

## Claims rule (hard)
Every capability is "what we are building" + its status from §5. Nothing in §5 is Live except the
demo; vouching, keys, forum are "in test"; all five properties are Planned. Never write that Gloki
*is* decentralized, secure, encrypted, private, or anonymous. Never "the only", "first", user
numbers, partners, testimonials, launch dates. No "Web5". "Blockchain" at most once, in the
builders section, and only as "not a blockchain in the crypto sense: no coins, no mining".
Quotes and numbers only from the research file; mark each one inline with `[check: A3]` style tags
so Eston can verify it on the source page. US-only figures must say "in the US".

## Voice
Plain, concrete, short sentences. A 16-year-old reading English as a second language must follow
it. Write like a person, not a brochure. Banned: em-dash chains, "not just X, but Y", "in a world
where", "imagine", "unlock", "empower" (as a verb in copy), "seamless", "revolutionary",
"game-changer", "harness", "leverage", "journey", "landscape", "robust", "cutting-edge", rhetorical
question openers, groups of three adjectives, exclamation marks, "we believe" more than once.
