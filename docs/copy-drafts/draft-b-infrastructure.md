## 2. The story

**Every vote starts with one question.** Before you count a vote, you need to know it comes from
one real person. Most online tools answer with an email address, a phone number or an
organiser's list [check: C]. Others use iris scans, passports or crypto deposits [check: C].

**Across borders, those answers break.** No single list covers everyone on Earth. Papers, face
scans or one company end up in the middle. We studied many tools. None of them combines one
person, one vote across borders, no central operator and a secret ballot [check: C].

**The hope was older.** In 1994, Al Gore spoke of "a kind of global conversation, in which
everyone who wants can have his or her say." [check: A3] The conversation came. A fair way to
count each voice did not.

**So we are building that part.** You get in because people who know you vouch for you. No ID
papers, no face scans, no tokens. Gloki does not do all of this yet. The board below shows what
works today and what is still being built.

---

## 3. What we're building

**One person, one vote** · *In test*
You get in when four people who know you vouch for you. No ID papers, no face scans, nothing to
buy or upload. This is built in the app and in test. Checks on the server side are still being
confirmed.

**Your records stay yours** · *Planned*
Each person has their own agent that keeps their records. A group shares only what belongs to
the group, and only with its members. Members do not run their own agents yet.

**A network its members carry** · *Planned*
Nodes run by members, not by one company. The node software is not ready yet. Later, where the
internet fails, messages could travel over radio. That part is still research.

## 6. For builders

Each person keeps a personal ledger, held by their agent. A contract runs only
among the people who are part of it, and is copied only between them. The design paper (Poupko,
Shapiro, Talmon) calls this subsidiarity. It is capital-free. It is not a blockchain in the
crypto sense: no coins, no mining. Identity comes from a web of trust: four members who know you
vouch for you. The weak points are written down. Today your key works like a password, writes
are not yet signed, and the paper notes that participants who collude can rewrite a contract's
history. If you know Solid, it is in that family. All of it is in test or planned.

## 7. Where this comes from

The Global Democracy Initiative began in 2021 as a coalition of organisations working for global
democracy. That coalition is no longer active. In 2023 some of us wrote a white paper, *Gloki:
Global Digital Voter Identity*. The beta has changed parts of it: now people who know you vouch,
not strangers chosen at random. GDI is a project of Young World Federalists, Inc., which hosts
it. Our old motto still guides us: "Assume global responsibility."
