## 2. The story

**The promise.**
In 1994, Al Gore described what the new networks could become: "Our goal is a kind of global conversation, in which everyone who wants can have his or her say." [check: A3] Many people shared that hope.

**What went wrong.**
We got the conversation. We did not get the say. In the US, crypto investment fraud took $7.2 billion from people in 2025. [check: B1] In the US, 52% of adults are now more concerned than excited about AI. [check: B2] Studies disagree on what feeds do to us. One experiment found that one week of re-ranked feeds changed how people felt about the other party. [check: B3]

**The turn.**
Technology is not going away. Global problems do not stop at borders. So we are building the good version.

**What we build.**
Gloki is our attempt to keep the 1994 promise. We are building it so that you get in because people who know you vouch for you, your records stay with your own agent, and members carry the network. Vouching is in test. The rest is planned, and we say which below.

---

## 3. What we're building

**One person, one vote** · *In test*
You get in because four people who know you vouch for you. No ID papers, no face scans, no tokens, nothing to buy. Vouching works in the app today and is in test.

**Your records stay yours** · *Planned*
We are building an agent for each person that keeps their own records. A group will share only what belongs to the group. Running your own agent is planned, not done.

**A network its members carry** · *Planned*
We want nodes run by members, not by one company. You will be able to host one on a small server. Later, where the internet fails, messages could travel over radio. That part is still research.

## 6. For builders

In our design, each person has their own personal ledger, kept by their agent. A contract runs only among the people who are part of it and is copied only between them. Ouri Poupko's paper calls this subsidiarity. It is not a blockchain in the crypto sense: no coins, no mining. Taking part needs no capital. If you know Solid, it is in that family. Vouching is in test in the app. Signed writes, protection against rewriting the record, a secret ballot, leaving with your identity and recovery through your vouchers are all planned, not built. The status board above is the honest list.

## 7. Where this comes from

GDI started in 2021 as a coalition of organisations working for global democracy. That coalition is no longer active. In 2023 some of us wrote a white paper, *Gloki: Global Digital Voter Identity*. The beta differs from it in places; where they differ, the beta is what counts. Today GDI is a project of Young World Federalists, Inc., which hosts it. Our old motto still holds: "Assume global responsibility."
