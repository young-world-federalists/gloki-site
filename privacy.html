Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The fonts and images come from this site itself, so your browser does not contact anyone else while you read it. The only personal data we ask for is your email address, and only if you join the waitlist.

The waitlist

When you join, we keep your email address and the way you chose to take part. They go to the Young World Federalists' account at Buttondown (Buttondown, Inc., United States), the service that sends our emails. We use them to write to you about Gloki and the Global Democracy Initiative, for example when the pilot opens, and for nothing else. We never sell or share them.

We rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email, or by writing to us. We keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). Like any web server, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address when you load a page, to run and protect the service. See GitHub's privacy statement. Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) runs the domain name, and forwards email you send to our address.

These providers are in the United States, as are the Young World Federalists.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, limit how we use it, object to its use, or get a copy. Write to the address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.
